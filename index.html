Provably fair

Each dive commits a hashed seed before you descend. The seed and every zone roll are revealed the moment the dive ends, so any outcome can be verified independently.
Concept prototype for evaluation only. Virtual credits with no monetary value. Total return to player is a verifiable 97% (3% house edge) that no descent strategy can beat: a 96% base dive plus a 1% contribution from every wager that funds the progressive jackpot, won only by surfacing from the deepest zone. The decision clock surfaces and banks your haul on timeout — it never advances you into further risk. Not a deployable real-money product: live play requires server-authoritative outcomes, independent certification, and licensing.
