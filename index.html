How to play FATHOM

v1.0.0 · RTP 96% + 1% ◆ jackpot · Provably fair · Free demo — virtual credits, no real-money play
◆ THE VAULT · 100× FLOOR

The goal

Dive as deep as you dare, then surface with your winnings before the dive crashes. The deeper you go, the bigger your multiplier grows — but the greater the chance you hit a hazard and lose everything at risk. Knowing when to stop is the whole game.

How to play

Hold the dive button to descend — your multiplier climbs the deeper you go. Let go to stop safely at any time, then Surface & Collect to bank your winnings. Push too far and a hazard ends the dive, taking everything still at risk.

The countdown clock: each time you stop, a short timer starts. Decide before it runs out — if it reaches zero you’re automatically surfaced and paid. It only ever cashes you out safely; it never dives you deeper, so hesitating can’t cost you your haul.

Bank Half locks in half your winnings while you keep diving with the rest — free insurance on a deep run.

◆ The jackpot

Reach the very bottom — the 100× floor — on a qualifying stake (50+ credits) and that 100× win is banked instantly. Then a free bonus dive into the Vault opens: ride it all the way to the floor and you win the entire jackpot pool on top. Come up short and you simply keep your 100× — the bonus risks nothing. The pool is shared by everyone and grows with every dive, climbing until someone cracks it.
